Where do you stand when the whole city turns gold?

A trip plan in six chapters — home, events, places, museums, food, cafés  ·  scroll ↓
가을
Before you go

Seoul in autumn runs on two colours: ginkgo yellow on the avenues, maple red in the palace gardens. This booklet collects everything saved for the trip — where you sleep, how you get there, which palace glows at night, and where to eat after.

One pattern shows up again and again in the notes: Monday. Seven of the places below close on Mondays, the lotus show skips Monday and Tuesday, and Odozip shuts on Sundays. Plan those days around the city's open doors — see The Week.

    Chapter VII — Day Trip

    A Day in Suwon수원

    A fortress wall, a mural village, two museums and a mall — about an hour south by train
    Planned for
    Fri 9 Oct
    Fri 9 Oct · Hangul Day · festival week

    The day trip lands on a public holiday, inside the Suwon Hwaseong Cultural Festival (수원화성문화제, Oct 4–11). A good day for the fortress, and the busiest day for crowds.

    • The festival runs 12:00–21:00 around Haenggung Plaza, Hwaseong Haenggung, Yongyeon and Yeonmudae. Free; a few booked programmes are paid.
    • Night shows: Yajo (야조), Oct 7–10, and Seonyumong (선유몽), Oct 5–11. Both are on for Fri 9.
    • The fortress wall and the mural village are open as usual. The art museum closes on Mondays only.
    • Samsung Innovation Museum is shut on public holidays. Go to Starfield instead.

    Deeper — Suwon on a holiday

    Leave Mangwon by about 9:00 and walk the wall before noon, when the festival crowds build. Haenggung-dong's cafés and restaurants will queue, so eat early or late. Decide before you go whether to stay for a night show — it sets which train you take back.

    Deeper — what to do on a Monday

    Monday is a good day for 63 Skypicnic, The Hyundai Seoul (its one October closing day is Mon 19), HAUS NOWHERE, Seongbuk-dong, the other cafés and most restaurants — check hours before going. Gwangjingyo's deck and the Suwon art museum are shut on Mondays. Sunday is the Jamsugyo festival; Odozip is shut that day.
